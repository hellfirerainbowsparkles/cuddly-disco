Because the Moon is tidally locked:

Rotation around its own axis: one rotation every 27.321661 days.
Orbit around the Earth: one orbit every 27.321661 days (sidereal period).

These periods are essentially the same, which is why the Moon always keeps nearly the same face pointed toward Earth.

For comparison:

Own-axis rotation at the equator: ≈ 16.7 km/h (4.63 m/s)
Own-axis rotation at 45° latitude: ≈ 11.8 km/h (3.27 m/s)

If you're instead asking about the Moon's orbital speed around the Earth, that's much faster:

Average orbital speed: ≈ 3,683 km/h (≈ 1.02 km/s).
